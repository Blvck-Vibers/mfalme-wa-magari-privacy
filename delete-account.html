Request account deletion

You can delete your Mfalme wa Magari account in the app or request deletion by email without opening the app.

Option 1: Delete in the app

  1. Open the Mfalme wa Magari application and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Delete Account & Data.
  3. Read the confirmation message and follow the prompts to request or confirm deletion.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email our support team from the email address associated with your account where possible. Use the subject Account Deletion Request – Mfalme wa Magari. Include your registered email address or phone number so we can locate the account. Do not send passwords, one-time codes or sensitive identity documents unless support provides a secure, necessary verification method.

What is deleted?

We process deletion of the account and associated personal profile information. Order, payment, invoice, shipping or customs records may be retained when required for legal, tax, accounting, dispute-resolution or security purposes. Where records must be retained, we restrict their use to those purposes. You can ask support what remains and why.

How long does it take?

In-app deletion follows the app's confirmation flow. For email requests, our team will review and respond to the request and may ask for reasonable account ownership verification. The time required depends on the records involved and any legal retention obligations.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Mfalme wa Magari handles information used to manage customer accounts and vehicle import orders.

Effective date: 10 November 2026  ·  Location: Dar es Salaam, Tanzania

1. Who we are

Mfalme wa Magari is a vehicle import management application associated with Mendez Cars. This policy applies to the Mfalme wa Magari mobile application and related customer support activities. For privacy questions, contact gabrieljoseph718@gmail.com.

2. Information we process

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number and authentication identifiers.
  • Vehicle and order information: vehicle details, chassis numbers, order status, shipping milestones, shipment references and documents relevant to vehicle importation.
  • Payment records: invoice amounts, payment status, outstanding balances, receipts and payment references entered or maintained for an order. The app does not itself process in-app card payments.
  • Support communications: information you provide when contacting support or requesting account deletion.
  • Technical information: limited device or diagnostic information may be processed by services used to operate and secure the app, depending on the features enabled.

3. How we use information

We use this information to authenticate users, display their vehicle orders, track shipment and customs-clearance milestones, maintain payment and invoice records, provide customer support, send relevant service notifications where enabled, secure the service and meet applicable record-keeping obligations.

4. Service providers and sharing

We use technical service providers to operate the application. Superbase is used for account-related functionality, including authentication and account deletion. Other services, such as cloud hosting, messaging or sign-in providers, may process information only where they are actually enabled in the app. We do not sell customer personal information. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or to support an authorized importation process.

5. Data security

We use access controls and secure network connections to help protect customer information. No online system can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their login credentials and contact us if they suspect unauthorized access.

6. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service and fulfill applicable legal, tax, accounting, dispute-resolution or security requirements. Some transaction or import records may need to be retained after an account is deleted. We limit retained information to what is necessary for those purposes.

7. Your choices and account deletion

Users can request account deletion within the app through Profile → Delete Account & Data, where that option is available. Users can also request deletion without using the app by following our Account Deletion instructions. We will process requests and explain any information that must be retained under applicable obligations.

8. Children

The application is intended for adults managing vehicle import orders and is not directed to children.

9. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, service providers or applicable requirements change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
